> We are proud to announce the official launch of the **Olympus Honor Legion**, the community of the **Felysyum Ecosystem**, on **September 26, 2026**.

The Olympus Honor Legion is more than a community. It is a place to **gather, share knowledge, connect, learn, grow, and build your Olympian title**.

---

### ⚡ MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS

Members of the **Olympus Honor Legion** will have access to exclusive opportunities across the Felysyum Ecosystem, including:

* 🎓 **SkillFullHub**: Access to special discounts and opportunities on educational lessons and learning programs.
* 🛍️ **Felyszone**: Enjoy **exclusive discounts and special offers** on products within the Felysyum marketplace.
* 💡 **Felynova**: Members may receive **special opportunities to have their innovative projects considered for funding** through the Felynova initiative.
* ❤️ **Aidora Charity**: Participate in **special charity opportunities and initiatives**, helping turn community strength into meaningful support for others.
* 🌐 **The Felysyum Ecosystem**: As the ecosystem continues to grow, Olympus Honor Legion members will be introduced to **additional privileges, opportunities, events, and community programs**.
